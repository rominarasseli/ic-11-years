How it all began

Eleven years ago, this office was a small team in Asunción with a big idea that broke the mold and crossed borders. But the story began a few years earlier, when Jessica Martin, our boss lady, took on the challenge of bringing a company based in South Dakota to Paraguay through remote work.

With the limited technology Paraguay had at the time, it seemed almost impossible. She did it anyway, with a big dream, a lot of hard work and a firm belief: that the right job in the U.S. could change a family's future, that every employer deserved to be connected with the right workers, and that every worker deserved an honest company guiding them, step by step.

Every number on this page started with one of you answering a call, reviewing a document, scheduling an appointment or staying late in the middle of a season. This is your story as much as the company's.

Departure2015Asunción

Where it all began

We opened our doors in Asunción, Paraguay, as International Connections S.R.L., with a small team and a lot of determination to connect workers with U.S. employers who needed them.

Entry2017

Going global

Our job offers reached farmers in South Africa, sheepherders in Mexico and truck drivers in Puerto Rico through local newspapers and magazines. We also held recruitment events in South Africa, meeting candidates face to face, with banners, brochures and IC caps ready on every table.

Long before design tools were easy to use and long before AI, this team designed everything by hand: application forms, PDF application templates, flyers, infographics and our yearly reports. We built a new online application system so every candidate lived in one place.

Community201910K

From 2,624 to 90,000+ followers

Our Facebook page grew with every season. Word of mouth became our strongest recruiter, and in 2019 we passed 10,000 followers. That season, 1,398 workers arrived in the U.S. with us. Today more than 90,000 people follow us.

Charter flights in a closed world

When the pandemic grounded the world, we didn't stop. Our H-2A workers traveled to the U.S. on charter flights (XX flights, XX workers) so farms could keep running and families could keep earning. Even that year, 1,489 workers arrived, more than the year before.

Still growing

2,288 workers from 32 countries arrived in the U.S., and 185 companies asked us to recruit for them, up from 110 the year before. Our visa denial rate stayed under 3%.

How we work together

Our culture

Big numbers come from small habits.

These are the things that make IC feel like IC, every single day.

Individuality

We celebrate who you are

Everyone brings their own style to the office. We promote acceptance, and we make room for fun.

Gratitude

We always say thank you

To each other, to our workers and to our employers. Gratitude is part of every day here.

Empathy

Kind words, always

We know that how we say things matters as much as what we say, so we keep everyone in the loop and speak to each other with patience and respect.

Our space

We treat the office like home

We care for our office the way we'd care for our own house: the garden, the pool, the walls and every piece of equipment. Nothing goes on a wall unless we know it will come off cleanly, we keep the pool clean and we call the gardener when it's needed. And we always do it as a team.

Our pets

We look after our furry coworkers

Beauty and the office pets have their own routine, and we make sure they're cared for on holidays and long weekends too.
